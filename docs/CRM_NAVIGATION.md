# CRM navigation

Choose a permitted area in the sidebar: Workspace, Voice Agent, Reports, Setup,
Twilio or Platform. The selected area's tools appear beneath it. **Find a tool**
searches across permitted areas; clearing search restores the selected area.
On mobile, open Menu to reach the same controls. Navigation retains visited
panels and unsaved drafts; see [Tabbed workspace](TABBED_WORKSPACE.md).

The business selector contains only authorized workspaces. An empty account
shows **No business workspace**, not a sample business or stale selection.
Global Users, Businesses, Websites and Twilio account directories require staff
access; business tools require their own grants. Forms-only users cannot read
SMS/submissions simply because they can edit a form.

Use **Forms** for named forms and their Automation/Submissions panels. Configure,
publish and enable a versioned sequence there. Use **Contacts → Manage
automations** for direct templates and enrollments, **Automation activity** for
outcomes, and **Inbound AI** for the independent inbound agent. The retired
fixed-group SMS menu/prompt workflow is not the current setup path. Email retains
its separate four-group workflow.

**Voice Agents** opens the connected/draft library and guided release panel.
**Service availability** configures operational booking capacity. The business
record's reviewed profile supplies facts, while written hours alone do not create
availability. See [Business profile form](BUSINESS_PROFILE_FORM.md),
[booking availability](BOOKING_AVAILABILITY.md) and [Customer Voice](CUSTOMER_VOICE.md).

The header logo returns to the CRM's retained workspace; forms-only accounts
return to their permitted Forms workspace. A missing control should be resolved
through intended permissions, not another person's identity. See the
[staff user guide](USER_GUIDE.md) for daily procedures.
